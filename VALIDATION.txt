SGHP case study: 200 controlled strategy trials plus two diagnostic bound
relaxations. See case_studies/sghp/RESULTS.md and baseline_summary.json.
Direct/staged success was identical on the tested feasible cases; the original
full-SGHP initialization difficulty has not been reproduced by the reduced loop.
Basic fallback continued all 16 low-flow trials rejected by the detailed valve.
These are accepted approximations, not validated detailed-valve solutions.
The fixed thermal budget limits conclusions about full-flowsheet relevance.

Complete SGHP study: 72 trials / 188 attempted solves, using all five exchangers
and four valves as selectable models with fixed phase-capable water/n-butane
properties. 57 final assignments accepted. Source-first and operating-context
strategies accepted 8/8 each; uniform all-high accepted 2/8. All-low accepted
6/8 and failed both low-demand starts. Source control-range conflicts were also
checked analytically without solving. See case_studies/sghp/FULL_STUDY.md,
FULL_RESULTS.md, full_baseline_summary.json, and data/full_source_control_range.json.
This is a complete topology reconstruction with calibrated rating assumptions,
not a historical control/initialization replay or independently validated plant.
